# Kid home

Scope: the child's home screen and the app shell it sets for every other screen. Mode: Operate. Audience: children 3 to 10 on their own phone or tablet; parents behind a PIN.

## Direction contract

THESIS: The balance is a glass jar of coins a child can read without reading. Refuses the dashboard of stat cards and progress rings that money apps ship.

OWN-WORLD: Money Jar. Mint ground tinted by each child's colour, dark green ink outlines 3 to 4px, flat candy fills (coral, violet, butter, gold coins), no blur, pill controls that sink when pressed, Grandstander lettering, authored cartoon mascots and job pictures.

STORY: A child sees how much they have, sees what they can do to earn more, taps "I did it", and later watches the coins land.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Avatar and streak chip on top. The balance number is the largest thing on the screen, full width, above the jar (owner's request: number gets visual priority and space). Below it a wide jar with the coin pile and the savings goal as a dashed line with a picture tag. Then a row of big picture job cards with an "I did it!" pill. Three-icon pill nav at the bottom.

FORM: Coin jar, position 1 on the grounded list (owner chose it over the assigned Coin Drop). seed:080f8e8d835479fb. Signature interaction: approved money drops into the jar as coins that bounce and pile, and the number ticks up; spending lifts coins out.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
